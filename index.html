Experience Alpine Elegance

Nestled in the heart of the Swiss Alps, The Aurelian offers an unparalleled retreat where timeless luxury meets breathtaking natural beauty.

Welcome to The Aurelian

Discover a world of refined mountain luxury where every detail has been curated for your comfort. From our world-class spa to gourmet dining, experience the pinnacle of Swiss hospitality.

Authentic Alpine Living

Our meticulously restored chalets blend centuries-old Swiss craftsmanship with modern amenities. Each residence features hand-carved wooden details, stone fireplaces, and panoramic windows that frame the majestic peaks beyond.

Winter Wonderland

When snow blankets the peaks, The Aurelian transforms into a pristine winter paradise. Ski directly from your doorstep to world-class slopes, then return to warm yourself by the fire with a glass of mulled wine.
